# Beme

> Beme Inc. (stylized "beme") was an American multimedia company founded in 2014 in New York City by
> YouTube filmmaker Casey Neistat and engineer Matt Hackett. Its mobile app let users capture and
> instantly share unedited 2-to-8-second videos without previewing them. CNN acquired Beme in
> November 2016; the app was shut down on January 31, 2017 and the company was folded into CNN
> Digital Studios on January 25, 2018. Beme is DEFUNCT and never published a public API.

## Status

- Company status: defunct (2014-2018)
- Public API: none. Beme never operated a developer program, API documentation, or developer portal.
- Domain: beme.com no longer belongs to Beme Inc.; it redirects to Hazel Health (https://www.hazel.co/),
  an unrelated company. Do not treat content at beme.com as a Beme artifact.
- Investors included Lightspeed Venture Partners and Vayner/RSE (seed, January 2015).

## Source code

- [github.com/beme](https://github.com/beme): the company's GitHub organization (New York City, created 2014-11-04).
- [beme/abide](https://github.com/beme/abide): a Go testing utility for HTTP response snapshots,
  Apache-2.0, published to the Go module proxy as `github.com/beme/abide`. Unmaintained since 2021.
  This is a testing library, not a client SDK for any Beme API.
